Meet Dr. Minerva Molon

      
More Than
A Diagnosis

      
Dr. Minerva Molon is a Diplomate and Fellow of the Philippine Psychiatric Association. She earned her medical degree at the UERM College of Medicine and completed her psychiatry residency at the National Center for Mental Health, the country's leading psychiatric hospital.

      
Her career reaches beyond the consultation room. She holds a Master in Public Health and served as Regional Director of the Department of Health for Eastern Visayas (Region VIII) before retiring. Leading health services for a whole region taught her to see the full picture: a patient's symptoms, but also their family, work and community.

      
Today she is a Visiting Consultant at the Department of Psychiatry of Eastern Visayas Medical Center and teaches future doctors and physical therapists at RTR Educational Foundation. In her own clinic she gives every patient unhurried time, a clear explanation of what is happening, and a treatment plan made together.

Before your visit

      
Questions From
Patients

      
Reaching out for the first time can feel like a big step. Here is what to expect.

      
You are not alone ♡

    

    

      
What happens at the first consultation?
Dr. Minerva will ask about what brings you in, your health history, sleep, work and family life. You can share as much or as little as you are comfortable with. At the end she explains her assessment and agrees a plan with you.

      
What is the difference between a psychiatrist and a psychologist?
A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who specializes in mental health. Psychiatrists can diagnose conditions, prescribe and manage medication, and coordinate care with therapists and other doctors.

      
Do I need a referral?
No referral is needed to book. If you have reports from another doctor or previous treatment, please bring them or send them before the visit.

      
How do online consultations work?
You receive a secure video link by email before the session. Join from a quiet, private place using a phone, tablet or computer with a camera.

      
Is everything I share confidential?
Yes. What you discuss stays between you and the doctor, except in the rare cases where the law requires otherwise, such as a serious risk to someone's safety. Dr. Minerva will explain this at your first visit.

      
Can I reschedule or cancel?
Yes. Please let the clinic know at least 24 hours ahead so the time can be offered to another patient.
